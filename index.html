About the show

Three friends. One pack. A whole world to discover.

From the sun-scorched dunes of the Sahara to the misty, emerald jungles of the Amazon, the Quack Pack travels to immersive places all over the globe. Every new destination sparkles with wonder. Ancient markets, whispering glaciers, coral gardens, and starlit savannas, where curiosity and learning hide around every corner.

They don't just visit these places. They listen, ask questions, and discover something new about the world and one another. Along the way, they quickly learn that together, there isn't anything they can't accomplish.

Meet the pack

For parents

What parents want to know.

Straight answers about content, age range, and what makes Quack, Quack, Pippin safe and worthwhile for young viewers.

What age is the show for?

Quack, Quack, Pippin is designed for kids ages 3 to 8. Episodes are rated TV-Y and built for preschool and early-elementary viewers, with simple language, clear story arcs, and a gentle pace suitable for the youngest audiences.

Is the show educational?

Yes. Every episode weaves natural curiosity into the story, including geography, ecosystems, problem-solving, and social-emotional skills like teamwork, empathy, and patience. Kids learn alongside the Quack Pack, not at them.

Where can I watch it?

New episodes are streaming on YouTube Kids. The platform offers parental controls, screen-time limits, and a curated feed designed for young viewers.

Is there any scary or violent content?

None. There's no violence, no scary villains, and no peril beyond what a curious duckling might find on a hike. Mild conflict is always resolved through teamwork and conversation.

How long are episodes?

Episodes are short by design, about 6 to 7 minutes each, to fit comfortably into young attention spans and parent-approved viewing windows.

What values does the show teach?

Friendship, curiosity, kindness, and the idea that being different is a strength. The Quack Pack's superpower is that they don't all think the same way, and they figure things out together.

Are the characters good role models?

Each character models a different positive trait: Pippin's curiosity, Chip's patience and craftsmanship, Sable's bravery balanced with care for friends. They make mistakes, apologize, and grow.

Does the show include diverse settings?

Yes. The Quack Pack travels everywhere from the Sahara to the Amazon to the Pacific coast. Every location is researched for accuracy and presented with respect, celebrating cultures, ecosystems, and ways of life around the world.
